# 2. PROJECT PURPOSE

Develop TMP Check, a scalable subscription-based service that helps traffic management businesses quality-check Traffic Management Plans.

The founding customer is Way Too Go Traffic Management.

WTGTM regularly outsources TMP proofreading. The business opportunity is to replace or substantially reduce this outsourced process through an AI-assisted checking service.

The product must provide a consistent, efficient and commercially attractive way to identify document errors, inconsistencies, missing information, technical concerns and potential compliance issues.

The product is intended to become a repeatable commercial offering that can eventually be sold to other traffic management businesses.

## Primary objective

Develop and validate a product that allows a customer to:

1. Subscribe to TMP Check.
2. Purchase checking credits.
3. Upload a TMP and its supporting documents.
4. Have the documents checked using the approved methodology.
5. Receive a separate checking report.
6. Receive an annotated copy of the original TMP.
7. Review historical checks and available credits.
8. Purchase additional credits as required.

The system must be designed for future scalability but must not be over-engineered before the first customer pilot proves the concept.

# 5. PRODUCT FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

TMP Check must check five principal areas.

## A. Proofreading and presentation
Spelling; grammar; punctuation; sentence clarity; formatting; headings and numbering; terminology; abbreviations; repeated or contradictory wording.

## B. Document consistency
Project names and numbers; client and contractor details; road names; locations and intersections; dates and times; work activities; traffic stages; document revision information; referenced drawings; cross-references; supporting attachments.

## C. Completeness
Missing information; blank fields; missing sections; incomplete tables; missing drawings; missing references; missing supporting documents; unanswered requirements; incomplete project details.

## D. Technical cross-checking
Compare written descriptions with supplied drawings, TGSs and supporting information. Identify potential inconsistencies involving: traffic staging; sign references; road and intersection descriptions; access arrangements; pedestrian and cyclist provisions; traffic control descriptions; worksite descriptions; contingency arrangements; references between the TMP and TGS documents.

Do not invent technical corrections or make unsupported assumptions about site conditions.

## E. Compliance observations
Check against the applicable, verified requirements and source library. Identify: potential non-conformances; missing required information; conflicting requirements; incorrect references; potentially outdated information; items requiring professional verification.

Every compliance finding must identify its source, edition/version and relevant clause or section wherever possible.

Do not claim that the document is compliant, approved or safe simply because it passes an automated check.

# 6. OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

Every completed TMP Check must produce two deliverables.

## Output 1 — TMP Check Report
A professionally formatted PDF containing: project identification; document details and revision; date checked; checking scope; compliance sources and versions; executive summary; findings by severity; page and section references; original text or identified issue; suggested correction or action; explanation; source reference where applicable; matters requiring human review; limitations and exclusions.

## Output 2 — Annotated original TMP
Provide a separate annotated PDF rendition of the submitted original. Requirements: preserve the original source document; highlight or mark relevant issues; assign finding reference numbers; link annotations to the report findings; preserve pagination and layout as far as reasonably possible; identify limitations where accurate annotation is not possible.

Never silently modify the customer's original document.

# 13. PILOT PLAN — WAY TOO GO TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT

Way Too Go is the founding customer and first commercial validation opportunity. The objective is to establish whether TMP Check can deliver a useful and reliable service that reduces dependence on outsourced proofreading.

## Phase 1 — Customer evidence
Obtain: (1) representative historical TMPs; (2) associated TGS drawings; (3) current WTGTM TMP templates; (4) relevant checklists; (5) previous proofreading corrections; (6) examples of issues identified by the existing proofreader; (7) customer-specific requirements; (8) approximate monthly TMP volume, currently unknown. Request anonymised documents or obtain explicit permission for use.

## Phase 2 — Reference test library
Create a controlled test set containing: known spelling and grammar errors; known consistency errors; missing information; known technical inconsistencies; known compliance concerns; correct examples that should not generate false findings. Have a qualified reviewer establish the reference findings.

## Phase 3 — Prototype
Build the checking engine and generate both outputs. Run historical documents through the system. Compare results against the reference findings. Record: correct findings; missed findings; false positives; incorrect source references; incorrect annotations; processing time; human review time.

## Phase 4 — Controlled live pilot
Start with a limited number of real TMPs. Every report receives human review before release. Collect customer feedback and refine the system. Do not remove the human review gate during the initial pilot.

## Phase 5 — Commercial validation
Measure: customer usage; average document size; checking time; human review time; processing cost; customer satisfaction; repeat usage; credit purchases; subscription retention; actual savings against the previous service.

The customer volume is currently unknown. Do not assume ten checks per month.
