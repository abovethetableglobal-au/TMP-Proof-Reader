## Purpose
Replace or substantially reduce WTGTM's outsourced TMP proofreading (benchmark $405/check, ~9 hrs) with a consistent, cheaper, AI-assisted check — then sell to other TM businesses.

## Objectives
1. Prove the checking engine produces accurate, useful, traceable findings (Gates 4–5).
2. Run a controlled, human-reviewed pilot with WTGTM (Gate 7).
3. Validate economics: delivery cost, review effort, willingness to pay (Gate 8).

## Scope — in (MVP, §11)
Five check categories (§5): proofreading; consistency; completeness; technical cross-check TMP↔TGS; compliance observations. Two outputs per check (§6): PDF report + annotated PDF of original. Severity framework (§7). WA compliance library (§8). Human review workflow (§9). PDF/DOCX/OCR intake (§10). Accounts, ledger, subscription, credits, history, admin (§11).

## Scope — out (deferred, §11)
Automated approval, engineering/TGS design or redesign, authority integrations/submission, mobile app, national coverage, advanced analytics, multi-company enterprise admin.
