**The problem**
Desk-checking a TMP takes hours: cross-referencing the WA Code of Practice, checking TGS sign sequences, matching risk registers to the drawings. A missed inconsistency means a resubmission, or worse.

**How it works**
1. **Upload** your TMP (PDF). Your original is never modified. (C-09)
2. **Check.** AI passes and coded rule checks run in about 8 minutes for an 81-page TMP in testing. (C-03)
3. **Review.** Your accredited reviewer accepts, edits or rejects each finding. (C-02)
4. **Issue.** Download the final report and the annotated TMP.

**What you get**
- A draft report rated Critical / Major / Moderate / Minor / Query.
- Every finding cites its source, edition and clause; anything without a citation is raised as a Query, never a finding. (C-01)
- An annotated copy of the TMP, page by page.
- Labels showing whether each finding came from both AI runs, one run, or a rule check. (C-08)
