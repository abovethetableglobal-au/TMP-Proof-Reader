**Conclusion:** a retail purchase lets a person read the standard. It does **not** permit loading it into TMP Check, AI processing, or reproducing its tables in the product. Both steps below are needed.

## Step 1 — buy the retail Web Reader (for the people who verify rules)
- Buyer: ATTG (business account), user: the accredited reviewer who verifies AS 1742.3 rules (or the Owner).
- Cost: $251.87 incl. GST.
- Use: a person reads clauses and writes **paraphrased rules with clause references** in TMP Check's rule library; no copying of text, tables or figures into the system.

## Step 2 — licensing enquiry (for use inside the product)
Ask permission to:
1. Store and process AS 1742.3:2019 within TMP Check (a hosted software service) so the checking engine, including an AI model operating under no-training terms, can compare customer TMPs/TGSs against its requirements.
2. Encode numerical requirements (e.g. sign spacing, taper lengths, device distances) as machine-readable rule parameters, with clause references, not displayed to customers in full.
3. Quote short extracts (clause number + brief requirement) in customer reports.
4. Use sign symbols/figures (if required) when annotating drawings.

## Interim position (until Step 2 is granted)
- AS 1742.3-dependent checks remain **"Unable to verify — AS 1742.3"** (A-35).
