## Email (informal request)

**Subject:** Request for statistical information — number of Traffic Management Plans received, 2021–2025

Dear [Team / Officer name],

I am researching traffic management activity in Western Australia and would be grateful for aggregate statistical information held by [Agency].

For each calendar or financial year from 2021 to 2025 (whichever is easier), could you please provide:

1. The number of Traffic Management Plans (TMPs) submitted to [Agency] for works on roads under its control.
2. The number of those TMPs approved or endorsed.
3. The number returned for amendment at least once (if recorded).
4. If available, a breakdown by works type (e.g. roadworks, utility works, events, building works) or by complexity (e.g. plans requiring Roadworks Traffic Manager endorsement).
5. [Councils only] The number of road reserve / obstruction permits issued that required a TMP.

I am requesting **counts only** — no plans, personal information or applicant details. Approximate figures or an existing report are entirely acceptable, and I am happy to receive whatever is readily available.

If this information is not available informally, please let me know whether a formal application under the *Freedom of Information Act 1992* would be appropriate.

Thank you for your time.
