# TMP Check — Reviewer test

**For:** Tara · **Time needed:** about 1 hour · **Where:** on a Teams/Zoom call; you control [Your name]'s screen

## What this is
TMP Check has run a draft check of the **SU 026 Shoalwater Foreshore** TMP (Rev 0). We'd like you to review every finding, as you would a proofreader's report, and we'll record how long it takes.
TMP Check does not approve anything. You decide which findings are right.

## Before the call
- [Your name] will text you your **sign-in email and password**.
- Join the call. [Your name] will share the browser and give you control.

## Steps
1. **Sign in** with your email and password.
2. Click **My Checks**, then open the top **SU 026 Shoalwater Foreshore**.
3. Say **"Start"**. [Your name] notes the time.
4. Work down the list. For each finding:

   | Button | Use when |
   |---|---|
   | **Accept** | The finding is right as written |
   | **Edit** | It's right but needs changing: type your correction in the box first, then click Edit |
   | **Reject** | It's wrong, or not worth raising |

   *Tip:* if you need to see the page, click **Annotated TMP** at the top. It opens the TMP with each finding highlighted and numbered.
5. When every finding has a decision, click **Issue final report**. Say **"Finished"**.

You can pause at any time; just say "pause" and "resume". Your decisions save as you go.

## Five quick questions afterwards
1. Were any findings plainly wrong?
2. Did it miss anything important?
3. Were there too many minor items?
4. Would the report and annotated TMP be usable for your work?
5. What one change would make the review faster?

Thank you.
