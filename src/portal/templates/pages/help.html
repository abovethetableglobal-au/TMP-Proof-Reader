Frequently asked questions

What does TMP Check do?

TMP Check reviews a Traffic Management Plan for proofreading, consistency, completeness, TMP-to-drawing cross-checks and selected Western Australian requirements, and reports potential issues with page references and source citations.

Is a TMP Check report an approval?

No. TMP Check is a document quality check. It is not an approval, certification, design review or safety declaration. Your accredited reviewer remains responsible for the professional assessment of the TMP.

Why is my report a draft?

Every report goes to your organisation's accredited reviewer first. The final report and annotated TMP are issued only after the reviewer has accepted, edited or rejected every finding.

When is a credit used?

One credit is used for each successfully completed check. Failed uploads or processing use no credit, and corrections to errors made by TMP Check are free. A re-check after you change your document uses another credit.
